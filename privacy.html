Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-10. Short version: there is almost nothing to collect, so nothing is collected.

1. This website

This site has no accounts, no analytics, no cookies and no tracking pixels. Downloads are served as static files. The only network calls the page makes are fonts, the version file on this same site, and the optional 2-reply demo described below. The demo keeps a small counter in your browser's local storage (2 free replies) and nothing else. It never leaves your device.

2. The 2-reply web demo

The demo box on the home page sends the message you type to a proxy that forwards it to an AI provider and returns the answer. The proxy rate-limits abuse (2 replies per visitor) and stores a short server log of demo turns. Do not type passwords, keys or personal data into the demo.

3. The desktop app

The Syncopx app runs on your PC. Chats, to-do items, notes and quick-job buttons are stored as files in your own Windows profile. API keys are encrypted with Windows DPAPI and only your Windows user can decrypt them. Nothing is uploaded anywhere: there is no Syncopx account and no Syncopx cloud.

4. Third parties

If you paste your own provider key into the app, your prompts go directly from your PC to that provider under their privacy policy. Font files load from Google Fonts when you visit this site, under Google's terms.

5. Contact

Privacy questions: reply through the channel where you found Syncopx.
